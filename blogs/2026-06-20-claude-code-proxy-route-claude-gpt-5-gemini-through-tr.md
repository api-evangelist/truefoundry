TrueFoundry AI Gateway acts as an enterprise proxy for Claude Code, letting developers route requests through a centralized control point across providers like GPT-5, Gemini, and Anthropic with cost visibility and controls. The gateway handles 350+ RPS on 1 vCPU with roughly 10 ms latency overhead, offering budget limits, rate limiting, and multi-model routing.
