TrueFoundry's AI Gateway addresses enterprise LLM cost optimization through centralized cost visibility, budget limits, intelligent model routing, and caching. The article claims 40-60% of token budgets in production LLM apps are pure waste, and cites typical savings of 30-50% through semantic caching and 60-90% through on-premises primary routing with cloud fallback.
