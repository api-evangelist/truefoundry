The 2026 Gartner Hype Cycle for Platform Engineering arrives as AI agents now participate as autonomous contributors in software delivery, agentic workloads create unpredictable cost structures, and governance models designed for human developers are becoming inadequate. The report examines 30+ technologies across the hype cycle's phases, emphasizing that platform decisions made in the coming year will determine whether organizations can scale AI responsibly. The analysis focuses on how platform engineering roles are shifting significantly and what enterprises should prioritize for AI governance at scale.
